Three quick steps and your coach is live. First you'll grab a free Google Gemini API key (that's what powers Vovo's brain — keeps it free), then connect Telegram.

2

Connect Vovo on Telegram

a) Open the bot: t.me/vovo_aharonenterprise_bot

b) Send /start, then connect your key: /key AIza... (Vovo deletes that message instantly and stores the key encrypted).

c) Set yourself up:

/goals Ship the client site · Gym 4x this week
/schedule 07:00 Workout | 09:00 Deep work | 22:00 Sleep
/status

That's it. Vovo takes it from there — daily check-ins, memory, and zero tolerance for excuses.

3

Use your Vovo web app

Send /web to the bot — it replies with a private button that opens your web app. Same coach, same memory as Telegram.

Timed reminders

Just say it: "I'll work out at 4" or "wake me every day at 6". At that exact time Vovo texts you on Telegram with ✅ / ⏳ 10 min / ❌ buttons.

Knowledge area

Teach Vovo about you — routine, diet, job, motivation. Add notes in the app or with /know .... It also learns from chat. Up to 25 notes.

Chat anywhere

Plan your day on the site, get pinged on your phone. Manage reminders with /reminders, set your timezone with /timezone.
