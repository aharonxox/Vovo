How to use Vovo

Just text it like a friend. No commands to remember.

It texts you on time

Say "I want to work out at 4" and your phone buzzes at 4:00. Say "every day at 7" and it repeats.

One tap to answer

When it checks in, tap ✅ On it, ⏳ 10 min or ❌ Skip. Changed plans? Say "cancel my gym reminder".

It gets to know you

Tell it about your job, routine, goals. It remembers — see and edit everything in the app under About me.

Your Vovo app
