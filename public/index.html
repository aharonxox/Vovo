How it works

Three commands. Zero excuses.

STEP 01

Set your goals

Send /goals once. Vovo stores them persistently and references them in every conversation.

STEP 02

Lock your schedule

Send /schedule with your routine. Vovo knows exactly when you said you'd be working.

STEP 03

Get audited

Vovo pings you 3× a day with one-tap accountability buttons. Answer honestly — it remembers.
